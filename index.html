Rain

Rain is a quiet idle game for iPhone about water, patience, and ambition. It begins with a single drop.

Rain on the App Store

Help

Questions, a bug, or something that looks wrong? Email schwadlabs@gmail.com and say what you saw and which iPhone you have.

Good to know

  • Rain 2.0 plays offline. There is no account and no sign in.
  • Your game is kept on your phone and saves as you play. Deleting the app deletes your game.
  • Progress from version 1.1 does not carry over to 2.0. Version 1.1 kept games on a server, and 2.0 has no server.
  • The silent switch quiets the game. Your own music keeps playing underneath.
